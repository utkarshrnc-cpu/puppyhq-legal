PuppyHQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2, 2026

PuppyHQ ("we", "our", "the app") is a pet management application. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you use PuppyHQ, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: Email address and display name, provided during sign-up via Apple Sign In, Google Sign In, or email registration.
  • Pet information: Pet name, breed, date of birth, weight, and other profile details you provide.
  • Health and activity data: Feeding logs, potty logs, grooming records, training sessions, vaccination records, symptoms, weight history, and veterinary contact information that you enter into the app.
  • Photos: Images you upload to your pet's timeline or profile.
  • Usage data: App interaction data used to improve the service.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the PuppyHQ app and its features.
  • Enable collaborative pet management between household members.
  • Generate AI-powered insights and recommendations about your pet's care.
  • Send push notifications related to your pet's care schedules and reminders.
  • Authenticate your identity and secure your account.

3. AI Features and Third-Party Processing

PuppyHQ uses OpenAI's API to provide AI-powered features such as care insights, pattern analysis, and the Ask Coach feature. When you use these features, relevant pet data (such as activity logs and health records) is sent to OpenAI for processing. This data is used solely to generate responses for you and is subject to OpenAI's Privacy Policy.

We do not send your email address or account credentials to OpenAI. Only pet care data necessary to generate the requested insight is transmitted.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a cloud-hosted platform built on PostgreSQL. Supabase provides:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS/SSL).
  • Row-level security (RLS) policies ensuring you can only access your own data and data shared with you through pet memberships.
  • Secure authentication via Supabase Auth.

Photos are stored in Supabase Storage with signed URLs that expire after a limited time.

While we take reasonable measures to protect your data, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following circumstances:

  • With other household members: If you invite others to co-manage a pet, they will have access to that pet's data.
  • With OpenAI: As described in Section 3, for AI feature processing only.
  • As required by law: If required by applicable law, regulation, or legal process.

6. Your Rights and Choices

You may:

  • Access, update, or delete your pet data within the app at any time.
  • Delete your account by contacting us, which will remove your personal data from our systems.
  • Opt out of push notifications through your device settings.
  • Choose not to use AI features, in which case no data will be sent to OpenAI.

7. Children's Privacy

PuppyHQ is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

AI-generated recommendations are not a substitute for professional veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Never disregard professional veterinary advice or delay seeking it because of something suggested by PuppyHQ's AI features.
